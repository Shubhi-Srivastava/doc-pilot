## Welcome
Welcome to AcmeSoft! This guide helps you navigate your first 90 days: access, tools, culture, and expectations. Your onboarding buddy will help you ramp up and connect with the team.

## Day 1 Checklist
- Pick up laptop and badge (if local).
- Log into email, Slack, HRIS, and the VPN portal.
- Complete security training and acknowledge key policies.
- Attend team standup and meet your manager.

## Week 1 Goals
Set up development environment, read engineering handbook pages, and pair with a teammate to ship a small change. Add your bio to the team page and schedule intro 1:1s.

## First 30 Days
Shadow on-call, learn the incident process, and ship a meaningful feature behind a flag. Create or update documentation to reflect what you learned. Participate in a retrospective.

## First 60–90 Days
Own a small project end-to-end, propose measurable success criteria, and present outcomes at the team demo. Identify a mentor and create a growth plan with your manager.

## FAQs
**Q: Where are architecture diagrams?** In the Engineering Wiki. **Q: How do I request hardware?** Submit an IT ticket under 'Procurement'.

## Best Practices Summary
- Complete security training and policy acknowledgments in Week 1.
- Pair with teammates and ship a small change in the first two weeks.
- Keep a running doc of questions and answers; contribute to the wiki.
- Schedule recurring 1:1s and set measurable 30/60/90‑day goals.
- Ask for feedback early and often; iterate visibly.
