AcmeSoft Internal Document — Data Privacy & Handling Policy
Last Updated: August 2025

---

## Scope & Principles
This policy governs how AcmeSoft collects, uses, stores, and disposes of personal data and company-sensitive information. We follow principles of lawfulness, fairness, transparency, purpose limitation, data minimization, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity and confidentiality.

## Roles & Responsibilities
The Data Protection Officer (DPO) oversees compliance. System Owners maintain records of processing activities. Employees must follow handling standards and report incidents promptly.

## Data Collection & Basis
Collect only what is necessary for legitimate business purposes, with a clear legal basis (contract, consent, legitimate interest). Provide notices explaining uses and retention.

## Storage & Access
Store data in approved systems with access controls based on least privilege. Encrypt Restricted data at rest and in transit. Monitor access logs for anomalies.

## Retention & Disposal
Apply retention schedules; when data is no longer needed, dispose of it securely via deletion, cryptographic erasure, or certified destruction. Suspend deletion during litigation holds.

## Data Subject Rights
Honor requests to access, rectify, erase, or port personal data where applicable. Verify identity before fulfilling requests and log responses.

## Third Parties & Transfers
Use processors under data processing agreements with appropriate safeguards. Conduct transfer impact assessments for cross-border data flows where required.

## Incident Management
Report potential breaches within one hour. The Incident Response Plan outlines containment, notification, and post-incident review steps.

## Best Practices Summary
- Collect only what you need; document purpose and legal basis.
- Apply least‑privilege access and review regularly.
- Encrypt sensitive data at rest and in transit; log and monitor access.
- Follow retention schedules; securely delete data when no longer needed.
- Verify identity before fulfilling data subject requests.
