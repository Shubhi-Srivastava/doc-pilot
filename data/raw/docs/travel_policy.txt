AcmeSoft Internal Document — Travel Policy
Last Updated: August 2025

---

## Booking & Approvals
Book travel through the approved agency to leverage negotiated rates and ensure duty-of-care coverage. Obtain manager approval before purchasing tickets. Consider carbon impact and combine trips when practical.

## Air Travel
Economy class is standard. Premium economy is permitted for flights over six hours with prior approval. Business class requires executive approval or medical accommodation.

## Lodging & Ground Transportation
Choose mid-range hotels close to the work site. Use public transport where safe; rideshare is acceptable late at night or when carrying equipment.

## Safety & Conduct
Follow local laws, keep valuables secure, and avoid high-risk areas. Alcohol consumption must be moderate and aligned with professional standards during business events.

## Expenses
Use the corporate card where available. Keep all itemized receipts. Currency conversion should match the card statement rate.

## Best Practices Summary
- Obtain manager approval before booking and use the approved agency.
- Choose economy fares and mid‑range hotels unless exceptions are approved.
- Keep itemized receipts and submit expenses within 30 days.
- Prioritize safety; follow local laws and company conduct standards.
- Combine trips where feasible to minimize cost and carbon impact.
