AcmeSoft Internal Document — Expense Reimbursement Guidelines
Last Updated: August 2025

---

## Overview
Employees are reimbursed for reasonable and necessary expenses incurred while conducting company business. This policy outlines eligible categories, limits, documentation, and approval flows.

## Eligible Categories
- Travel: airfare (economy), baggage, ground transport.
- Lodging: standard business hotels.
- Meals: within per diem limits; alcohol not reimbursable unless client-required.
- Equipment: peripherals with manager pre-approval.
- Conferences: registration with manager and budget approval.
Employees are encouraged to choose cost-effective options and book in advance when possible.

## Submitting Expenses
Submit via the Expense Portal within 30 days of the expense date. Attach itemized receipts showing date, vendor, and amount. Convert foreign currency using the rate on the transaction date.

## Approvals & Audits
Managers review for business purpose and policy adherence; Finance conducts random audits. Repeated violations may result in denial or disciplinary action.

## Per Diem & Limits
Standard meal per diem is $60/day domestically and $75/day internationally. Hotel limits follow city-specific caps published quarterly.

## FAQs & Examples
**Example**: A two-day trip with flights, hotel, and meals. Submit receipts for airline, hotel folio, and itemized restaurant bills. **Q:** Can I upgrade to premium economy? **A:** Only with manager approval for flights over 6 hours.

## Best Practices Summary
- Book travel early and choose cost‑effective options.
- Upload itemized receipts within 30 days; add business purpose notes.
- Use the corporate card when available; avoid personal purchases.
- Verify exchange rates and taxes on international receipts.
- Ask Finance about exceptions before spending, not after.
