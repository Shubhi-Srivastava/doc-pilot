AcmeSoft Internal Document — Employee Code of Conduct
Last Updated: August 2025

---

## Respect & Inclusion
Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. We foster an inclusive culture and do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, or bullying.

## Integrity & Ethics
Act honestly, avoid conflicts of interest, and protect confidential information. Gifts and entertainment must be modest and accurately reported.

## Professional Conduct
Use company resources responsibly, keep accurate records, and comply with all laws and policies. Report violations to HR or the Ethics Hotline.

## Reporting & Non-Retaliation
We investigate reports promptly and prohibit retaliation against good-faith reporters. Confidentiality is maintained to the extent possible.

## Enforcement
Violations may result in corrective action up to termination. Severe violations may be reported to authorities as required by law.

## Best Practices Summary
- Treat everyone with respect; zero tolerance for harassment or retaliation.
- Disclose potential conflicts of interest promptly.
- Protect confidential information and follow data handling rules.
- Report concerns to HR or the Ethics Hotline in good faith.
- Model professional conduct at client sites and company events.
