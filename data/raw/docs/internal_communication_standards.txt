AcmeSoft Internal Document — Internal Communication Standards
Last Updated: August 2025

---

## Principles
Communicate with clarity, kindness, and context. Default to transparency when appropriate, and document decisions in durable places.

## Channels
Slack: real-time collaboration; Email: formal approvals; Confluence: documentation; Jira: work tracking; Zoom/Meet: video conferencing. Choose the channel that matches the urgency and permanence of the message.

## Writing Guidelines
Lead with the action or decision, then provide background. Use headings, lists, and short paragraphs. Avoid jargon and unexplained acronyms. Include owners and due dates.

## Meetings
Send agendas 24 hours in advance, start on time, and end early when possible. Capture notes and action items; assign owners. Prefer asynchronous updates to recurring status meetings.

## Response Time Expectations
Slack during local working hours: within 2 hours. Email: within 1 business day. Escalations: use the on-call rotation or phone as documented.

## Best Practices Summary
- Lead with the decision or ask; add context below.
- Use the right channel for urgency and permanence (Slack vs. email vs. docs).
- Write clearly with headings and bullets; avoid unexplained acronyms.
- Share agendas 24h in advance and capture actions with owners and dates.
- Default to transparency when appropriate and document decisions.
