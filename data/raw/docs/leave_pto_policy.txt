AcmeSoft Internal Document — Leave & PTO Policy
Last Updated: August 2025

---

## Purpose
This policy explains how paid time off (PTO) and leaves of absence work at AcmeSoft, including eligibility, accrual, usage, and approval processes. It ensures coverage while supporting employee wellbeing.

## PTO Accrual & Carryover
Full-time employees accrue PTO each pay period based on tenure tiers. Up to five unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year; additional carryover requires HR approval for exceptional circumstances.
Accrual Tiers:
- 0–2 years: 15 days
- 3–5 years: 20 days
- 6+ years: 25 days

## Requesting Time Off
Submit requests via the HRIS at least two weeks in advance when possible. Managers balance business needs and fairness. Approved PTO is visible on team calendars to coordinate coverage.

## Sick Leave & Medical Appointments
Sick leave is separate from PTO and may be used for illness, medical appointments, or to care for a family member. For absences over three consecutive days, HR may request a doctor's note.

## Holidays & Floating Days
AcmeSoft observes company holidays published annually. Employees receive two floating holidays for cultural or personal observances.

## Unpaid Leave & Protected Leave
Certain leaves (parental, medical, bereavement, jury duty) may be paid or protected according to applicable laws. Contact HR to initiate a case; documentation may be required.

## Travel & PTO During Emergencies
If emergency events (natural disasters, outages) occur, managers may cancel or reschedule PTO to maintain critical operations, with HR ensuring equitable treatment.

## FAQs
**Q: Can I cash out unused PTO?**
A: Only where required by law or specified by local policy.

**Q: What if my PTO request conflicts with team coverage?**
A: Managers will try to accommodate alternatives and maintain fairness.

## Best Practices Summary
- Submit PTO requests at least 2 weeks in advance when possible.
- Coordinate with teammates to maintain coverage during your absence.
- Use sick leave for health-related time off; provide documentation if requested.
- Review your accrual balance before booking travel.
- Record handover notes and on-call swaps before extended leave.
